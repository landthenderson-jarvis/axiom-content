Common Questions

What's the difference between wearable and video baby monitors? Wearable monitors, like the Owlet Smart Sock, track vital signs such as heart rate and oxygen levels directly from the baby. Video monitors, on the other hand, provide a visual feed of your little one's surroundings.

Can I use a baby monitor with multiple cameras? Yes, many modern baby monitors allow you to connect multiple cameras, offering a comprehensive view of your home or specific areas.

What's the importance of motion detection in a baby monitor? Motion detection helps reduce false alarms and ensures that you receive only critical notifications about your baby's movements. This feature is particularly useful for parents seeking real-time updates on their little one's activity levels.
