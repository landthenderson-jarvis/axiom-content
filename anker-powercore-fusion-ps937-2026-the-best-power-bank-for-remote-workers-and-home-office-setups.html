What to Look For

  • Capacity: look for at least 15,000mAh; anything less may not be enough for your needs
  • Charging speed: prioritize a power bank with USB-C or QuickCharge (QC) support for fast charging

  • Durability: opt for power banks with solid construction and UV-resistant materials
  • Compatibility: ensure the power bank is compatible with your devices' charging protocols
