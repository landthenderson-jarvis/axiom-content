Common Questions

What is deterministic routing?

Deterministic routing refers to the ability of a router to ensure that data packets are processed in a predictable and reliable manner. This is critical for applications like online gaming or 4K streaming, where latency and packet loss can significantly impact performance.

How do I prioritize traffic on my network?

You can prioritize traffic on your network using the QoS feature on your router. This allows you to assign priority levels to specific devices, applications, or types of traffic, ensuring that critical data packets are processed first.

What is MU-MIMO support?

MU-MIMO (Multi-User Multiple Input Multiple Output) support refers to a router's ability to serve multiple devices simultaneously, enhancing overall network efficiency and performance. This is particularly useful in high-bandwidth applications like 4K streaming or online gaming.
