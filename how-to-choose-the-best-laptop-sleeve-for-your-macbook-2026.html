Troubleshooting

Sleeve Not Securing Properly

Cause: Incorrect application of 3M Adhesive Tape. Fix: Reapply the tape, ensuring a secure hold by pressing firmly along the edges. If the issue persists, re-clean and re-tape the area.

Fabric Strips Falling Off

Cause: Insufficient adhesive or poor fabric quality. Fix: Reinforce the strips with additional adhesive dots or replace them with new ones if necessary.

Sleeve Not Repelling Water

Cause: Incorrect application of Silicone Spray or low-quality product. Fix: Reapply the spray, ensuring a thin, even coat. If the issue persists, switch to a different brand or type of silicone-based waterproofing solution.

Common questions

How do I ensure the sleeve doesn't leave residue on my MacBook?

To avoid leaving residue, use a soft cloth to wipe down the area before applying the sleeve. If residue persists, try using a gentle cleaning solution or consulting the manufacturer's guidelines for removal.

Can I customize the sleeve with custom artwork or designs?

Yes! Many manufacturers offer customization options for their sleeves. Check with Artisyn or other brands for specific details on design and printing processes.

Will this sleeve protect my MacBook from physical damage?

While the sleeve provides some protection, it's not designed to withstand extreme forces like drops or crushing. Handle your MacBook with care, and consider additional precautions such as a laptop stand or case for added protection.
