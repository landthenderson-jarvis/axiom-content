What to Look For

When shopping for a Fujifilm pocket-sized camera, here are some key specs to consider:

  • Sensor size: Look for cameras with APS-C sensors or larger for better low-light performance.
  • Lens quality: Check the maximum aperture and optical zoom range for flexibility.
  • Autofocus: Ensure your camera has fast and accurate autofocus capabilities.
  • Video: If you plan on shooting video, look for cameras with 4K resolution and stabilization.
  • Battery life: Aim for at least 20 hours of battery life to minimize downtime.
