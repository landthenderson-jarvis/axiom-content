What to Look For

  • Water resistance: look for at least 300m (1000ft) rated; most budget options top out at around 50m
  • Movement: opt for automatic or quartz-powered; mechanical movements are pricier but offer more character
  • Materials: stainless steel, titanium, or high-quality leather straps are great options; avoid cheap plastics and low-grade metals
  • Additional features: consider chronograph functions, date displays, or GPS tracking depending on your needs

Common Questions

What's the best watch for a pilot?

The Breitling Navitimer 41mm is an excellent choice for pilots and adventure-seekers. Its chronograph function and slide-rule bezel make it perfect for calculating flight times and data.

Can I wear a watch with formal attire?

Yes! Many of our Quick Picks, such as the Cartier Tank Solo Watch or the Rolex Air-King 40mm, are suitable for formal occasions.

Are automatic watches more reliable than quartz-powered ones?

Generally speaking, yes. Automatic movements tend to be more durable and require less maintenance than quartz-powered movements. However, it's essential to research specific models and brands to ensure you're getting a high-quality timepiece.
