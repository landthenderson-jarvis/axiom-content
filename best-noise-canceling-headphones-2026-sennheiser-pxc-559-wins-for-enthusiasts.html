Common Questions

How do I maintain the noise-canceling headphones' battery life?

To extend your headset's battery life, keep it in a cool, dry place and avoid exposing it to extreme temperatures. Regularly cleaning the ear cups can also help maintain performance.

Yes! All of our top picks are compatible with multiple devices via Bluetooth or wired connections.
