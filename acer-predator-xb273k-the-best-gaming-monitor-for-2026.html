What to Look For

  • Panel type: TN (twisted nematic) for fast response times, IPS (in-plane switching) for color accuracy, or VA (vertical alignment) for good contrast ratios.
  • Refresh rate: 144Hz or higher for smooth gaming performance.
  • Response time: 5ms or lower for minimal ghosting and blurring.
  • HDR support: if you want to take advantage of high dynamic range visuals.
  • Connectivity: at least one HDMI port, DisplayPort, and USB ports for connecting your devices.

Common Questions

How do I choose the right gaming monitor?

When selecting a gaming monitor, consider your budget, the type of games you play, and the features that matter most to you. Look for a monitor with a high refresh rate (144Hz or higher), fast response time (5ms or lower), and good color accuracy.

What's the difference between IPS and TN panels?

IPS (in-plane switching) panels offer better color accuracy and wider viewing angles than TN (twisted nematic) panels. However, TN panels are generally faster and more affordable.

Can I use my gaming monitor for other tasks?

Yes! A good gaming monitor can double as a display for your computer or TV, making it a versatile addition to any setup.
