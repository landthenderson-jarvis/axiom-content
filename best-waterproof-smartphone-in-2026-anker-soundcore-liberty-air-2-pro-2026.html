What to Look For

When searching for a waterproof smartphone, remember:

  • Battery: look for 20+ hours rated; budget options often lie by 30%
  • Water resistance: aim for IPX7 or higher
  • Durability: check the materials used and its overall build quality
  • Storage: ensure it has at least 128GB of storage space
  • Processor: opt for a mid-range to high-end processor for smooth performance
