Common Questions

What is deterministic routing?

Deterministic routing refers to the ability of a router to ensure that packets are transmitted in a specific, predictable sequence, reducing latency and improving overall network performance.

How do I choose the right router for my needs?

Consider your online activities, such as gaming, streaming, or general browsing. Choose a router with sufficient processing power, memory, and Ethernet ports to handle your demands.

Can I use multiple routers together?

Yes, many modern routers support mesh networking, allowing you to connect multiple devices seamlessly. This is especially useful for large households or areas with weak Wi-Fi signals.
