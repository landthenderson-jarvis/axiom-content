Who Should Buy This

The Canon EOS Rebel T8i is perfect for anyone who wants a high-quality DSLR that won't break the bank. If you're a beginner, this camera is ideal for learning the ropes and producing great content. What to Look For

  • Sensor: look for 24MP+ resolution; anything lower will struggle in low light
  • Autofocus: opt for Dual Pixel AF or similar technology for fast and accurate tracking
  • Video: seek cameras with 4K resolution and at least 30fps frame rate
  • Battery life: aim for 20+ hours rated battery life; budget options often lie by 30%
  • Weather-sealing: consider cameras with weather-resistant bodies for outdoor use
