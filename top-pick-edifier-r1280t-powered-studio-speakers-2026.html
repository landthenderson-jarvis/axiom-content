What to Look For

When shopping for bookshelf speakers, keep in mind:

  • Frequency response: Aim for 50-20,000 Hz or higher.
  • Power output: Opt for at least 10W RMS per channel.
  • Connectivity options: Ensure compatibility with your devices (e.g., Bluetooth, USB).
  • Size and design: Consider the space constraints of your room and desired aesthetic.
