What to Look For

When shopping for a device that can help you tune out online noise, consider the following:

  • Noise-cancellation: Look for devices with active noise-cancellation (ANC) technology that can effectively block out ambient and AI-generated noise.
  • Frequency response: A frequency response of 20Hz-20kHz is ideal for most users, ensuring clear and balanced sound.
  • Battery life: Aim for at least 15 hours of battery life to minimize recharging interruptions.
  • Comfort: Opt for devices with comfortable ear cups and adjustable headbands to ensure long-term wearability.
