Who Should Buy This

The Neato Botvac D7 Connected is perfect for those living in apartments or small spaces, especially with pets. Its advanced navigation system makes it easy to maneuver around furniture and avoid obstacles.

What to Look For

  • Battery life: look for at least 60 minutes of continuous cleaning; rechargeable batteries are a must.
  • Suction power: aim for 1000+ Pa (pascals) for effective dirt removal.
  • Navigation system: opt for advanced mapping and navigation features for efficient cleaning.
  • Noise level: choose robots with quiet operation or adjustable sound levels.
  • Compatibility: ensure the robot is compatible with your smartphone or tablet.
