Who Should Buy This

If you're an eco-conscious adventurer who prioritizes sustainability and durability, the JBL Clip 4 is a must-have. Its green credentials are impressive, with a recyclable design and minimal waste packaging.

On the other hand, if you're looking for something more portable or budget-friendly, consider the UE Wonderboom or Anker Soundcore Flare 2 instead. These options might not offer the same level of eco-friendliness but are excellent choices for casual use.

What to Look For

  • Battery: look for 20+ hours rated; some budget options often exaggerate by 30%
  • Water resistance: IPX7 or higher is a must for outdoor use; don't settle for less
  • Materials: opt for eco-friendly materials like recycled plastic, bamboo, or sustainably sourced wood
  • Size: consider compact designs for easy portability and storage
  • Price: expect to spend around $100-$200 for high-quality options
