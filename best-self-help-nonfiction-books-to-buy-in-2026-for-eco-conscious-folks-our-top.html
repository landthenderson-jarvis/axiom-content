Common Questions

What is the most eco-friendly way to read a self-help nonfiction book?

The most eco-friendly way to read a self-help nonfiction book is by borrowing it from your local library or purchasing an e-book version. If you prefer physical copies, look for books with high levels of post-consumer waste and carbon offsetting.

Can I still buy these books if they're not available on Amazon?

Yes, you can purchase these books through other online retailers like Barnes & Noble, Indiebound, or directly from the publisher's website. Be sure to check the book's eco-friendliness before making a purchase.

Are e-books more sustainable than physical copies?

While e-books do have a lower carbon footprint due to reduced production and transportation costs, they also require energy-intensive servers for storage and distribution. However, many e-book platforms are working towards increasing their sustainability by using renewable energy sources and reducing waste.
