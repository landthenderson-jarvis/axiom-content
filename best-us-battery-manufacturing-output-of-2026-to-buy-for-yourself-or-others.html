What to Look For

When shopping for batteries, keep an eye out for:

  • Rated runtime: Aim for at least 20 hours of power; some budget options might claim 30+ but fall short.
  • Capacity: Higher-capacity batteries tend to be more expensive, but they'll last longer in devices that drain quickly.

  • Price: Be wary of extremely low prices; high-quality batteries often cost a bit more.
