Common Questions

What is the best mechanical keyboard for gaming? The Logitech G915 TKL is an excellent choice for gamers who want a premium gaming experience without breaking the bank. Its fast and responsive keyboard with customizable backlighting options make it ideal for long gaming sessions.

How do I clean my mechanical keyboard? For basic cleaning, use compressed air to remove any debris from between the keys. For more thorough cleaning, mix equal parts water and white vinegar in a spray bottle and gently wipe down the keyboard.

What is the difference between Cherry MX Brown and Cherry MX Red switches? Cherry MX Brown switches provide a smooth and tactile feedback, while Cherry MX Red switches are linear and quiet. The choice ultimately comes down to personal preference.
