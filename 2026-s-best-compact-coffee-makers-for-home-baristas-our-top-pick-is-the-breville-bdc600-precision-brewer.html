What to Look For

  • Compact design: look for machines under 12 inches in width
  • Adjustable brew strength: aim for at least 4-5 levels of customizable brewing
  • Automatic shut-off: ensure your machine turns off after a set period of inactivity
  • Temperature control: target machines with adjustable temperature between 195°F and 205°F
  • Cleaning ease: prioritize machines with easy-to-clean features like removable grinds containers
