What to Look For

When shopping for a laptop sleeve or MacBook case, look out for:

  • Water resistance: Ensure the case meets IPX4 standards or higher.
  • Shock absorption: A 5-6 inch drop test should be feasible without damage.
  • Durability: Check for high-quality materials and stitching that can withstand regular use.
  • Customization options: Some cases offer different colors, designs, or inserts to personalize your device.
