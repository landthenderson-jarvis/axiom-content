Common Questions

What's the best way to get started with AI development?

We recommend starting with a beginner-friendly board like the Intel Nuc Compute Card or Google Coral Dev Board. These boards provide an easy-to-use interface and extensive documentation to help you get started.

Can I use my existing computing setup for AI development?

Unfortunately, most AI development requires powerful processing capabilities that your average laptop or desktop can't handle. You'll need a dedicated board like the NVIDIA Jetson Xavier AGX Development Kit (TX2) or Xilinx Zynq-7040 SoC to get started.

What's the difference between edge AI and cloud AI?

Edge AI refers to the development of AI models on devices with limited processing power, such as cameras or sensors. Cloud AI refers to the training and deployment of AI models in the cloud using powerful servers. The Xilinx Zynq-7040 SoC is perfect for edge AI applications.
