What to Look For

  • CPU: look for at least 4 cores and 8 threads for efficient processing
  • GPU: aim for at least 2 GB of VRAM and 1280 CUDA Cores for intense graphics processing
  • RAM: ensure a minimum of 16 GB for smooth performance
  • Storage: opt for at least 256 GB SSD for fast data access
  • Power consumption: consider kits with low power draw for energy efficiency
