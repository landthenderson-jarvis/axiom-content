Common Questions

How do I choose the right AI dev platform for my project?

Start by identifying your project's specific needs and requirements. Consider factors like processing power, storage capacity, operating system, and connectivity options to ensure you select a board that meets your demands.

Can I use an Arduino Uno Rev3 for industrial-scale applications?

While the Arduino Uno is an excellent starting point for beginners, it may not be suitable for industrial-scale projects due to its limited processing power (16 MHz). Consider more powerful boards like the Raspberry Pi 4 Model B or the Mycrochip PIC32MX795F512L for larger-scale applications.

What's the difference between Wi-Fi and Bluetooth capabilities?

Wi-Fi enables wireless communication over short distances, while Bluetooth provides connectivity over shorter ranges. Consider your project's specific needs and choose a board with the appropriate connectivity options.
