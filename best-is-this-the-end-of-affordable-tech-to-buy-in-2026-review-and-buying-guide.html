What to Look For

When shopping for affordable tech in 2026, look for:

  • Processor: Intel Core i3 or i5 for smooth performance

  • Storage: 256GB SSD for fast loading times and responsiveness
  • Display: FHD (1080p) resolution for crisp visuals; consider OLED displays for enhanced contrast
  • Battery Life: Aim for at least 6 hours of continuous use
