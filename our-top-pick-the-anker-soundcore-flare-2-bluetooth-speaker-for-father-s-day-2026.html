Who Should Buy This

The Anker Soundcore Flare 2 is perfect for dads who want a reliable wireless speaker that can withstand rough handling. If you're looking for a speaker to take on camping trips, beach days, or pool parties, the Flare 2 is an excellent choice.

On the other hand, if your dad prefers high-end sound quality and doesn't mind spending more, the Libratone Zipp might be a better fit. However, keep in mind that it's pricey and not as durable as the Flare 2.

What to Look For

  • Battery life: look for at least 10 hours rated; anything less is a deal-breaker
  • Water resistance: if your dad loves water sports or outdoor activities, make sure the speaker is waterproof (IP67 rating or higher)
  • Sound quality: bass response and clarity are key; avoid speakers with muffled sound
  • Durability: look for rugged designs that can withstand rough handling
  • Price: set a budget and stick to it; wireless speakers range from under $50 to over $300
