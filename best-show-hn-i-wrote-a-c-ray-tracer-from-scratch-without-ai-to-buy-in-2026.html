Common Questions

What is ray tracing, and how does it improve gaming performance?

Ray tracing is a technology that allows for more realistic graphics by simulating the way light behaves in the real world. It improves gaming performance by reducing aliasing, motion blur, and other visual artifacts, creating a more immersive experience.
