When shopping for an eco-friendly smartwatch, look for: * Eco-friendly packaging: made from recycled materials * Sustainable materials: biodegradable, recyclable, or repurposed materials * Energy efficiency: low-power consumption and long battery life * Water resistance: suitable for outdoor use and water sports * Durability: resistant to scratches and drops
