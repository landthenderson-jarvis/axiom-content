What to Look For

When shopping for AI-designed radio chips, make sure to consider the following:

  • Range: Look for chips with a range of at least 5 meters (16 feet) to ensure reliable connectivity.
  • Power consumption: Choose chips that are designed for low power consumption to minimize battery drain.
  • Compatibility: Ensure that the chip is compatible with your desired smart home platform or IoT standard.
  • Development tools: Consider the availability and ease of use of development tools, such as APIs and SDKs.
