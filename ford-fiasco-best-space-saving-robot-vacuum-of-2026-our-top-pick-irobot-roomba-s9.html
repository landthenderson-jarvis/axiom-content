What to Look For

  • Battery life: look for at least 60 minutes of runtime on a single charge.
  • Suction power: a minimum of 1,000 Pa (pascals) is recommended.

  • Noise level: if you plan to use the robot vacuum during the night or early morning, look for one that operates quietly - below 60 decibels.
