What to Look For

When shopping for a monitor, keep the following in mind:

  • Response time: look for 5ms or lower for smooth gaming and minimal ghosting.
  • Color gamut: aim for 100% sRGB or higher for excellent color accuracy.
  • Brightness: consider a minimum of 300 cd/m² for comfortable viewing in bright environments.
  • Connectivity: ensure it has multiple ports, including HDMI, DisplayPort, and USB.
  • Stand: opt for an adjustable stand with height and tilt adjustments for optimal comfort.

Common Questions

What is the best monitor for gaming?

Our top pick, the Dell UltraSharp U2421H, offers excellent color accuracy and decent gaming performance. However, if you're looking for a more serious gaming monitor, consider the ASUS VG248QE for its fast response time and low input lag.

What is the difference between IPS and TN panels?

IPS (In-Plane Switching) panels offer better color accuracy and wider viewing angles compared to TN (Twisted Nematic) panels. If you're looking for excellent text clarity and color reproduction, consider an IPS panel.

Will a higher resolution monitor improve my overall computing experience?

Yes! A higher resolution monitor can greatly enhance your viewing experience with sharper text and more detailed images. However, keep in mind that a higher resolution may require more system resources to run smoothly.
