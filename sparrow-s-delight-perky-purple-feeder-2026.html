What to Look For

When choosing the best smart bird feeder for your small space, keep these key factors in mind: * Seed capacity: Aim for at least 1/2 pound of seed to minimize refills. * Sensor sensitivity: Ensure accurate detection and tracking with a minimum sensitivity range of 0.5-1 foot. * Power source: Opt for battery-powered feeders with 20+ hours of runtime or solar-powered options for eco-friendly operation. * Durability: Look for weather-resistant materials and sturdy construction to withstand harsh conditions.
