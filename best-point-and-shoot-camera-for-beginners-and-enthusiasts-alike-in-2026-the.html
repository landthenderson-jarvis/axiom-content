Who Should Buy This

The Canon G7 X III is perfect for anyone looking for a high-quality point-and-shoot camera that can keep up with their active lifestyle. If you're a beginner, this camera will help you improve your photography skills quickly, while enthusiasts will appreciate its advanced features and excellent image quality.

On the other hand, if you're on a tight budget or don't need all the bells and whistles, consider the Fujifilm XP140 or Olympus TG-6. These cameras are more affordable and still offer great image quality, but with fewer features.

What to Look For

  • Sensor: look for at least 16 megapixels; higher is better
  • Lens: f/1.8 or wider for improved low-light performance
  • Image stabilization: optical or electronic IS can help reduce camera shake
  • Video resolution: 4K or full HD (1080p) for high-quality footage
  • Battery life: at least 20 hours rated; budget options often lie by 30%
