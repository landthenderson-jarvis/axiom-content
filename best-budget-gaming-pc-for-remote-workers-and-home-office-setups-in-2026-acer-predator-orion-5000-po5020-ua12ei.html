What to Look For

When shopping for a budget gaming PC, make sure it meets the following criteria:

  • CPU: look for at least 10th Gen Intel Core i3 or AMD Ryzen 5
  • RAM: ensure at least 8GB of DDR4 memory
  • Graphics Card: opt for NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650 or higher
  • Storage: choose a PC with at least 256GB SSD storage
  • Display: consider a PC with an integrated display if you don't have a separate monitor
