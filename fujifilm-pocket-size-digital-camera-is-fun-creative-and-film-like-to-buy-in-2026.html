What to Look For

When shopping for a pocket-sized digital camera, be sure to look for:

  • A minimum 20MP sensor for high-quality images
  • Fast autofocus with advanced features like eye-tracking or phase-detection
  • Image stabilization to reduce camera shake and blur
  • Weather-sealing or water-resistance for added durability
  • A touchscreen LCD for intuitive control
