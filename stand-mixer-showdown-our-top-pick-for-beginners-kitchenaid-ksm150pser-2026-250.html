What to Look For

When shopping for a stand mixer, consider the following features: * Bowl size: look for at least a 5-quart capacity for making large batches of dough or batter * Speeds: 10 speeds or more are ideal for precision and control * Power: a powerful motor with at least 300 watts is necessary for heavy-duty use * Controls: easy-to-read controls and a quiet operation are must-haves * Attachments: look for included attachments like a whisk, dough hook, and paddle
