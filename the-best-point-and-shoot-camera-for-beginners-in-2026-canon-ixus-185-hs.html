Who Should Buy This

The Canon IXUS 185 HS is perfect for beginners who want an easy-to-use point-and-shoot camera. It's ideal for capturing everyday moments, vlogging, and taking stunning photos of friends and family. If you're a seasoned photographer looking for something more advanced, consider the Sony WX800 or Panasonic LX15 instead.

On the other hand, if you're on a tight budget, the Fujifilm FX-10 is a great option. However, keep in mind that it may lack some features and performance compared to the top-tier cameras.

What to Look For

  • Image sensor: look for at least 1/2.3" sensor size; anything smaller may produce softer images
  • Video resolution: opt for 4K or Full HD (at least 1080p) for smooth, high-quality footage
  • Autofocus: choose a camera with fast and accurate phase-detection autofocus for sharp photos
  • Zoom: consider a camera with at least 5x optical zoom; more is better for flexibility
  • Battery life: look for cameras with at least 20 hours of battery life to avoid frequent recharging
