Who Should Buy This

This guide is perfect for tiny apartment dwellers who want to get outside without sacrificing their living space. If you're a couple or solo adventurer, these compact camping gear picks are designed specifically with small spaces in mind.

On the other hand, if you have ample storage and prefer more spacious options, consider investing in larger tents or opting for separate storage solutions.

What to Look For

  • Waterproof rating: look for at least IPX3 (10-minute rain resistance) and consider IPX4 (30-minute rain resistance) for heavier downpours
  • Setup time: aim for under 5 minutes; some models take up to 15 minutes, so be sure to research before buying
  • Pack size: consider tents that pack down to under 12 inches tall or less
  • Weight: if you're planning a backpacking trip, prioritize lightweight options under 3 lbs
  • Ventilation: look for tents with built-in vents and mesh panels to ensure airflow
