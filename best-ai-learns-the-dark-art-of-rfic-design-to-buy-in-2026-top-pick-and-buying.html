What's the Difference Between Analog and Digital ICs?

Analog ICs like the ones on this list are designed to manipulate analog signals, whereas digital ICs are used for manipulating digital signals. When choosing between an analog and digital IC, consider what type of signal you're working with and what your design requirements are.
