Who Should Buy This

This guide is perfect for anyone seeking a durable and reliable computer that can handle demanding workloads. If you're a professional looking to upgrade your workstation or need a laptop for heavy-duty tasks like video editing or 3D modeling, look no further.

However, if you're on a tight budget or only require basic computing tasks like browsing and office work, consider our budget pick instead.

What to Look For

  • Processor: Opt for at least an Intel Core i7 with a clock speed of 2.5GHz or higher.
  • RAM: Ensure the laptop has at least 16GB of DDR4 memory, but we recommend 32GB or more for optimal performance.
  • Storage: Choose a device with a Solid-State Drive (SSD) as the primary drive and consider adding an external hard drive for bulk storage needs.
  • Battery Life: Aim for at least 12 hours of battery life, but be aware that this may impact overall performance.
