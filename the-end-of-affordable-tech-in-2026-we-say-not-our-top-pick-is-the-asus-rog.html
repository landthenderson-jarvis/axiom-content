What to Look For

  • Processor: look for at least an 11th Gen Intel Core i5 processor.
  • Memory: aim for at least 16 GB of RAM; budget options often come with 8 GB or less.
  • Storage: opt for a solid-state drive (SSD) rather than a hard disk drive (HDD).
  • Battery Life: expect at least 10 hours rated battery life.
  • Graphics Card: look for a dedicated NVIDIA GeForce GPU.
