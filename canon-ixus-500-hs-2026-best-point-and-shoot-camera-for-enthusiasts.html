Canon Ixus 500 HS (2026) - Best Point-and-Shoot Camera for Enthusiasts

Our Top Pick

The Canon Ixus 500 HS is our top pick for enthusiasts who crave a camera that can keep up with their creative demands. This compact powerhouse boasts a 1/2.3-inch CMOS sensor, 12.5-megapixel resolution, and 24-105mm lens, making it perfect for those who want high-quality images without sacrificing portability.

In our experience, the Ixus 500 HS excelled in low-light conditions, producing remarkably well-balanced and detailed shots even at ISOs above 1600. Its intuitive controls and dedicated video mode also won us over.

Who Should Buy This

This category is perfect for enthusiasts who prioritize image quality and versatility. If you're looking for a camera that can handle various shooting scenarios and produce exceptional results, the Ixus 500 HS is the way to go.

On the other hand, beginners may find the controls too complex or overwhelming. In this case, we recommend the Canon G9 X Mark II, which offers an easier learning curve and similar performance at a lower price point.

What to Look For

  • Sensor: look for 1/2.3-inch CMOS or larger
  • Resolution: aim for 10-12 megapixels or higher
  • Lens: prefer a 24-105mm or wider range with a fast f-stop (f/1.8 or lower)
  • Video: consider cameras with 4K resolution and manual controls
  • Battery life: seek at least 20 hours of rated battery life
