What to Look For

  • Print resolution: Aim for 300x300 dpi or higher for crisp, detailed prints.
  • Paper usage: Opt for printers that use refillable paper packs or recycled materials.
  • Battery life: Look for at least 20 hours of battery life; budget options might claim 30%, but you'll be lucky to get half that.
  • Compatibility: Ensure the printer is compatible with your smartphone or camera's operating system.
