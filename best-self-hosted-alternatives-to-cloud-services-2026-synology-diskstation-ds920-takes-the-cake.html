What to Look For

When selecting a self-hosted alternative to cloud services, consider the following:

  • CPU: look for at least 1.5 GHz dual-core or quad-core processors
  • RAM: aim for 2GB or more for smooth performance
  • Storage: opt for at least 4TB of storage space for media and data backup
  • Compatibility: ensure compatibility with your existing devices and software
  • Power consumption: choose NAS solutions with low power consumption (<20W) to reduce energy costs
