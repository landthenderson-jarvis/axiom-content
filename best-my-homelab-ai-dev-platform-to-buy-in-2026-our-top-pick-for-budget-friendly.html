What to Look For

When shopping for an AI development platform, make sure to check the following:

  • Processing Power: Look for at least 1GHz processor; anything less may struggle with complex AI tasks.
  • Memory: Aim for 2GB RAM or more; this will help prevent memory constraints during development.
  • Storage: Ensure there's ample storage space (at least 8GB) to accommodate your projects.
  • Connectivity: Opt for a platform with expandable GPIO pins and USB connectivity for easy integration with other devices.
