Common Questions

What if my lawn has a unique issue that the device can't diagnose?

In this case, you may want to consider consulting with a professional or purchasing additional accessories (e.g., soil testing kits) to help identify and address the problem.

Can I use these devices on slopes or uneven terrain?

Most devices are designed for flat lawns. However, some models (like the GreenEye Pro) have specialized sensors that can handle slight inclines. Always check the product specifications before using it on uneven terrain.

How long do I need to recharge my device between uses?

Recharging times vary depending on the device and its battery life. Check your device's documentation or manufacturer's website for specific guidance.
