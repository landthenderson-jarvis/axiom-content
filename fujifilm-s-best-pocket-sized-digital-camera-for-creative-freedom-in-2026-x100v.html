Who Should Buy This

The Fujifilm X100V is perfect for:

  • Photographers who want exceptional image quality without sacrificing portability

On the other hand, if you're looking for a more straightforward point-and-shoot experience with advanced features, consider one of the Quick Picks. The X100V's learning curve can be steep, especially for beginners.

What to Look For

When choosing your new compact camera, keep an eye out for:

  • Sensor resolution: 24MP or higher for high-quality images
  • Autofocus system: hybrid or phase-detection systems provide faster and more accurate focusing
  • Video capabilities: 4K or higher for smooth footage and advanced features like slow-motion recording
  • ISO range: 1600-6400 to handle low-light conditions without sacrificing image quality
  • Lens aperture: f/1.8 or wider to create a shallow depth of field and improve low-light performance
