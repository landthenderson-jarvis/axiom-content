About 22 Tanks

We're an independent team of hobbyist engineers, home lab builders, and long-time tech enthusiasts. 22 Tanks publishes detailed buying guides focused on AI hardware, local LLM infrastructure, home lab servers, and the consumer tech that actually holds up over time.

Every guide starts with real research: benchmarks, user reports, spec sheets, and honest comparisons. We name specific models and prices, acknowledge real weaknesses, and skip the filler. The goal: we do the homework so you don't have to.

Our focus areas:

  • AI & Home Lab — GPUs for local inference, home servers, NAS builds, Raspberry Pi alternatives, self-hosted tools
  • Consumer Tech — laptops, earbuds, monitors, and the gadgets genuinely worth your money

We participate in the Amazon Associates program and earn a small commission on qualifying purchases at no extra cost to you. This never influences our recommendations — we only recommend products we'd buy ourselves.
