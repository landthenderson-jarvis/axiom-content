Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

22Tanks Incorporated ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website 22tanksinc.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit the Site.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to operate the Site:

  • Usage data — standard web server logs including IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referral source. This data is used solely for analytics and is not sold.
  • Cookies — we use cookies for site analytics (Google Analytics) and affiliate tracking (Amazon Associates). You may disable cookies in your browser settings.

Affiliate Links

22tanksinc.com participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program. When you click product links and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. Amazon may collect data per their own privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect data independently:

  • Google Analytics — website traffic analysis. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdSense — advertising. Google Privacy Policy
  • Amazon Associates — affiliate links. Amazon Privacy Policy
  • Pinterest — content distribution. Pinterest Privacy Policy

How We Use Information

Information collected is used to:

  • Understand how visitors use the Site in order to improve content
  • Track affiliate referrals for commission attribution
  • Display relevant advertisements via Google AdSense

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We share data only with the service providers listed above, each of whom operates under their own privacy policy.

Data Retention

Server logs are retained for up to 90 days. Analytics data is retained per Google's standard retention settings (14 months by default).

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable privacy law (including GDPR and CCPA) to access, correct, or delete data we hold about you. To make a request, contact us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy
