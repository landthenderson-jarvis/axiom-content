Common Questions

What's the best way to clean these gadgets?

For most devices on this list, a gentle soap and water solution is sufficient. For non-stick surfaces, avoid using abrasive cleaners or scouring pads.

Can I use these gadgets for cooking raw meat?

Yes, all of these devices are capable of cooking raw meat to safe internal temperatures. However, always follow the manufacturer's guidelines and ensure that the device is properly cleaned and sanitized before handling raw meat.

How do I know which gadget is right for me?

Consider your meal prep goals, the type of food you want to cook, and your countertop space constraints when choosing a gadget. If you're unsure, start with a more versatile device like the Instant Pot and add specialized gadgets as needed.
