Common Questions

What does the term "hydration" mean in skincare?

In simple terms, it refers to how well a product helps retain moisture within the skin. Look for products claiming 12+ hours of hydration.

Is it true that natural ingredients are better for my skin?

Not necessarily – some synthetic actives can be more effective or gentle than their natural counterparts. Be aware of your skin type and concerns, then choose accordingly.

Can I use these products on sensitive skin?

Some products, like La Roche-Posay's Toleriane Ultra Fluid, cater specifically to sensitive skin. Always patch test a new product before committing to a larger size or long-term use.
