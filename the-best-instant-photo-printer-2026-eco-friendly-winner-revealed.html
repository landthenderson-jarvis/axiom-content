  • Eco-friendliness: Look for printers that use recycled ink cartridges and reduce waste.
  • Battery Life: Opt for printers with a battery life of at least 20 hours to ensure you can print on-the-go.

  • Connectivity: Ensure the printer has Wi-Fi connectivity and is compatible with your smartphone or tablet for easy printing.
