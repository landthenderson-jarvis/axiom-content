What to Look For

When shopping for eco-friendly snap AR glasses, make sure to prioritize:

  • Battery life: look for devices with a minimum of 10 hours rated battery life
  • Materials: opt for brands using recycled or biodegradable materials whenever possible
  • Durability: choose glasses with IPX7 or higher ratings for water resistance and shock tolerance
  • Compatibility: verify compatibility with your preferred device ecosystem (e.g., Android, iOS)
  • Carbon footprint: research the company's sustainability initiatives and emissions reduction efforts
