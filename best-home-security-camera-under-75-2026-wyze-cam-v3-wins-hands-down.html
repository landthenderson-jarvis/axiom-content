Who Should Buy This

If you're a renter or have a small space, the Wyze Cam v3 is perfect for you. It's easy to set up, doesn't require any wiring, and provides excellent video quality.

What to Look For

  • Sensor: Look for cameras with at least 720p resolution and a wide-angle lens (around 100°).
  • Night Vision: Ensure cameras have infrared LEDs or night vision sensors for clear footage in low-light conditions.
  • Motion Detection: Opt for cameras with adjustable sensitivity levels to minimize false alerts.
  • Power Source: Consider cameras with rechargeable batteries or plug-in options for added flexibility.
  • Cloud Storage: If you want cloud storage, look for cameras offering 1-year or more recording retention.
