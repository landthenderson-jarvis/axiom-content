What to Look For

When shopping for the best NAS for your home lab, look for:

  • Processor speed: at least 1.8 GHz quad-core CPU
  • RAM: minimum 4 GB DDR4 (8 GB or more recommended)
  • Storage capacity: starting from 2 TB (expandable options available)
  • Network interface: Gigabit Ethernet and/or dual-band Wi-Fi
  • Compatibility: ensure compatibility with your operating system of choice
