What to Look For

When shopping for a pocket-sized digital camera, look for:

  • A sensor size of at least 1/2.3 inches for good low-light performance.
  • A battery life of at least 10 hours for extended use on the go.
  • A resolution of at least 12 megapixels for high-quality stills and video.
  • A lens with a focal length of at least 24mm ( equivalent) for wide-angle shots.
