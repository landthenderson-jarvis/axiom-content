Who Should Buy This

The Sony RX0 II is perfect for beginners who want to dip their toes into the world of photography without breaking the bank. It's also an excellent option for enthusiasts who need a reliable backup or a secondary camera for specific situations. However, if you're looking for advanced features like 4K video or RAW image capture, you may want to consider one of our other picks.

On the other hand, if you're an enthusiast looking for a high-end point-and-shoot camera with advanced features and exceptional image quality, the Fujifilm X100V is the better choice. However, be prepared to pay a premium price for this top-notch device.

What to Look For

  • Sensor size: look for at least 1/2.3-inch sensor for decent low-light performance
  • Resolution: aim for 12-16 megapixels for high-quality images
  • ISO range: choose cameras with an ISO range of 100-6400 or higher
  • autofocus: opt for cameras with phase-detection AF or contrast-detection AF with face detection
  • battery life: look for cameras with at least 20 hours rated battery life
