What to Look For

  • Navigation: Opt for vacuums with advanced navigation systems, such as infrared sensors or AI-powered mapping.
  • Suction Power: Aim for at least 10 airwatts of suction power to effectively pick up pet hair.
  • Battery Life: Choose vacuums with batteries rated for 20+ hours to ensure you can clean multiple rooms without interruption.
  • Noise Level: If you plan to use your vacuum during office hours, look for models with noise levels under 60 decibels.
