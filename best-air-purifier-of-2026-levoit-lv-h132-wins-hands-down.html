What to Look For

When shopping for an air purifier, keep the following in mind:

  • CADR: look for at least 100 CFM (cubic feet per minute) for efficient cleaning
  • Filter replacement: consider filters that can be replaced every 6-12 months or less
  • Noise level: if you plan to use it during sleep or work, opt for a model with a low decibel rating (<30 dB)
  • Certifications: look for products certified by reputable organizations like AHAM or LEED
