What to Look For

  • Speed: look for routers with speeds reaching at least 1Gbps on both the 2.5GHz and 5GHz bands
  • Range: ensure your router has a minimum range of 200 feet to cover large homes or offices
  • Devices supported: look for routers that can handle at least 100 devices connected simultaneously
  • MU-MIMO support: consider routers with multi-user multiple input multiple output (MU-MIMO) support for improved performance
  • Tri-band WiFi: if you have a lot of devices on your network, consider a router with tri-band WiFi for better performance
