What to Look For

  • Processor: Opt for at least a Core i5 or AMD Ryzen 5 equivalent.
  • RAM: Ensure it has 8GB or more of DDR4 memory.
  • Storage: Prioritize SSDs (512GB+), as they greatly improve performance.
  • Battery Life: Aim for 10+ hours rated, and be wary of budget options that often exaggerate their battery life.
  • Display: Look for a Full HD (1920x1080) or 4K resolution panel with good color accuracy.
